Text Search

🔍 Searching handwritten text

A sample text ("hello my friend") is loaded automatically on startup — no need to write anything to try the search.

The search works on the JIIX export, which contains each recognized word with its bounding box (position and size in mm). Matching words are highlighted by overlaying a yellow <div> at the corresponding coordinates.
The search is case-insensitive and supports multi-word phrases — all consecutive words must match in order.
You can also write your own text and search within it. Use Convert to turn the handwriting into digital text.

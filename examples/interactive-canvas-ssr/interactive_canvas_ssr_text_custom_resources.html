📦 Custom pre-loaded resources

Unlike an inline custom lexicon, resources are uploaded to the recognition server in advance — they can be full vocabulary lists or language models, pre-processed and ready to use.

Once uploaded, reference a resource by its identifier in customResources. Multiple resources can be combined.
This example loads a dinosaur resource — try writing Triceratops, Brachiosaurus, or Pachycephalosaurus.
As with the custom lexicon, the engine evolves: some of these words may eventually be recognized without a resource.

∑ Handwritten math recognition — MathML export

Write any mathematical expression — fractions, integrals, square roots, Greek letters, matrices, sums, limits… The engine recognizes a broad range of standard notation.

The recognized expression is exported as MathML (application/mathml+xml) with the standard flavor, making it directly compatible with Microsoft Office, LibreOffice, and any MathML-aware renderer.
The result panel shows both views side by side: rendered MathML on the left (native browser) and the raw MathML markup on the right for copy-paste into your application.

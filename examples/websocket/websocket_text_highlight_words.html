🖍️ Ink color & word highlighting

Pick a color from the palette, then write — ink is drawn in that color. Switch colors between words to mix inks.

To highlight a word, circle it with the current pen color. The circle color becomes the word's highlight background.
The panel on the right lists every highlighted word with its ink color and highlight color — updated live on each recognition export.
Technically, the JIIX export is requested with style: true. Circled words appear in jiix.spans with class text-highlight; the span's style field carries color (ink) and -myscript-text-decoration-background-color (surround color).

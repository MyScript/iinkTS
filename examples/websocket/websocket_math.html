Websocket Math

∑ Handwritten math recognition

Write any mathematical expression — fractions, integrals, square roots, Greek letters, matrices, sums, limits… The engine recognizes a broad range of standard notation.

The recognized expression is exported as LaTeX (application/x-latex) and rendered live above using KaTeX. Any LaTeX-compatible renderer (MathJax, KaTeX…) can be plugged in the same way.
The solver is enabled (solver.enable: true) — simple arithmetic expressions are evaluated automatically.
Use Convert to finalize the recognition and replace the ink with clean typeset math.

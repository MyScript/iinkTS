📖 Custom lexicon

The recognition engine may struggle with rare or highly technical words — think internal project codenames, proprietary product names, uncommon surnames, or domain-specific abbreviations your team coined.

A custom lexicon gives the engine a hint: words listed there become valid candidates during recognition, even if absent from the built-in dictionary.
As the engine continuously improves, some of these words may eventually be recognized without a custom lexicon.
Type your words space-separated in the field below and click Send — the editor reloads with the new lexicon applied.
